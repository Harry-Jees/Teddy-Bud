# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Teddy Bud aims to be a respectful, welcoming community for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and viewpoints. We want technical discussion to be constructive and safe, particularly because the project concerns private conversations and emotional support.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback and correcting mistakes.
- Explain disagreements calmly and welcome beginner questions.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's private messages, identifying details, credentials, or sensitive information without permission.
- Use inclusive language and keep feedback relevant to the project.
- Follow the repository's contribution and security-reporting guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized or hateful content directed at other community members.
- Doxxing, unwanted disclosure of private information, or attempts to access accounts or systems without authorization.
- Deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith conduct, or abusive communication.
- Public disclosure of an unpatched vulnerability or exploitation instructions that put users at risk.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related public communications.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report harmful behavior to the project maintainer through the contact channel listed on the GitHub profile or repository. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Include links and enough context to investigate, but do not redistribute private or sensitive material.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and confidentially where practical. Responses may include a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces, depending on severity and context. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
