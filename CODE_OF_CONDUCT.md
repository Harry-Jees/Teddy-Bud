# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Teddy Bud welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's personal information or confidential data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related public interactions. Report problems privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary.
