## The product

Teddy Bud is a calm, personal AI companion for talking things through, reflecting, and returning to conversations that matter. It should feel warm and reassuringâ€”not like a generic AI dashboard, a clinical tool, or a childish character app.

The product principle is **â€œA little room to breathe.â€** The interface uses a soft, mature claymorphism language with Material 3 foundations, warm ivory and cocoa tones, clear hierarchy, purposeful motion, and an 8-point spacing rhythm. Keep the experience uncluttered and accessible. There is no dark mode.

Teddy Bud is an AI companion, not a therapist, emergency service, or substitute for professional care. Responses can be mistaken or incomplete.

## Product requirements

- **Conversation first:** natural text conversations, streaming responses, history, and clear retry/error states.
- **Contextual Teddy stickers:** nine separate bundled sticker poses can accompany an assistant reply when the moment fits; they are not a user-facing sticker picker and are suppressed for crisis-support conversations.
